# VAUG showreel: 60-second script

**Who it's for:** accelerators we want to partner with, so their portfolio startups bring us software and growth work. It speaks to startups and enterprises alike.
**Goal:** introduce VAUG as one AI-first team with 120+ clients that builds and grows, then prove it with live product screens from real client work.
**Format:** 16:9, 1920×1080, 60s, with music. The voiceover is a separate track, already synced.

## Putting the video together

1. Open the film and click **Export** to download the MP4. The music is included.
2. Lay the voiceover over it. Either hand the MP4 to Claude Code, which mixes the voice over the music (music ducked under every line) and writes a 1080p H.264 file, or use any editor (CapCut, iMovie, Premiere, DaVinci Resolve): put the MP4 and `vaug-showreel-voiceover.m4a` on their tracks at 0:00.
3. Export. Every voiceover line already starts on its scene, so nothing needs nudging.

## Look

- VAUG's dark theme: background `#131116`, violet `#8b5cf6`, yellow `#ffd23f` for the numbers that matter, Geist type.
- **120+ clients, everywhere:** in the company stats, on the section tag of every success story, as the headline of the stories wall, on the accelerator line and on the end card, and twice in the voiceover.
- Every client beat shows that client's own product screens, taken at full resolution from its case study on vaug.in, so each one looks different and carries the client's own brand.
- **Pages within pages:** each screen is filmed like a screen recording. The cursor glides to the key figure or link and clicks it, the camera dives into the click, and the next page of that product opens with a yellow ring on what matters there (for example Onsi's 52s opens a bound policy; Traxpay's 92.4% opens an exception with the AI agent's explanation).
- Think3D and Amity open a leads inbox in their own colours, with new leads landing at the top and the count ticking up. These two inboxes are redrawn for the film and labelled so; their rows are illustrative.
- CBD2Heal and CareerNaksha use their real report screenshots from the proposal. Figures inside the product screens are the site's illustrative data, labelled on screen; the headline figure on each callout is the case study's result. No Google, Meta, HubSpot, Linear or WhatsApp screens are copied.
- Music: warm electric piano, marimba and a light kit at 120 BPM. Every cut lands on a beat, each client screen lasts one bar, and every click, page swoop and ring has its own sound.
